# Evidence, Probability and Research Protocol

## Evidence grades

- S: multiple independent direct sources align
- A: strong evidence with more than one supporting source
- B: meaningful support but plausible alternatives remain
- C: weak, single or indirect evidence
- D: insufficient; normally exclude from headline conclusions

## Pollution risk

- P0 blind analysis
- P1 partial background known
- P2 extensive history known; confirmation bias risk
- P3 outcome known; hindsight reconstruction risk

## Model confidence

- M0 almost no information
- M1 limited information
- M2 moderate, multi-event information
- M3 multiple settings and repeated patterns
- M4 longitudinal records across major contexts

## Timeline

Use a timeline for sudden breakups, suspected infidelity, third parties, overlapping relationships, prolonged withdrawal or conflicting accounts.

| Time | Event | Source | Confidence | Decision relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Check statement-behaviour, statement-statement and responsibility-shifting contradictions.

## Probability

Use probabilities only when multiple plausible explanations matter to the decision.

- State probability: a psychological state may exist
- Causal probability: a factor caused an event
- Outcome probability: a future event may occur

Give broad ranges and confidence, not fake precision. Mutually exclusive explanations should roughly sum to 100%; overlapping states need not.

Every important forecast should include a time window, observable outcome, current range, update conditions and later calibration. Do not use probability as decoration.

## Key-message forensics

When the actual message is available, distinguish explicit language from missing language and inspect finality, ambivalence, affection, guilt, contempt, blame, distancing, responsibility, uncertainty, future orientation, structural complaints, intimacy, family, repair invitations and regret markers.

Absence of language is not proof of absence of feeling.

## External research

Use current authoritative sources for medical, legal, crisis or exact statistical claims. Name whether a source is empirical research, clinical framework, popular psychology or anecdotal community evidence.

### Reddit and community evidence

Community accounts can reveal possible experiences and strategies, not prevalence or individual prediction.

Explicitly note:

- self-selection and survivorship bias
- unverifiable identity and timeline
- platform ranking bias
- people who regret a breakup may post more than people who do not
- one-, three- and six-month stories are not universal psychological stages

Separate outputs into: what self-reports illustrate, what research supports at group level, and what case-specific behaviour supports. Never convert Reddit frequency into a probability for one person.
