# Legal and Safety Mode

Use this mode for stalking, harassment, violence, threats, coercive control, intimate-image abuse, fraud, property disputes, severe financial exploitation or credible self-harm/violence risk.

Priority:

1. Immediate safety
2. Trusted person and safe location
3. Preserve evidence without escalating contact
4. Identify jurisdiction
5. Verify current official law and services
6. Consider formal reporting, protective, medical and legal options

Do not provide generic emergency numbers or helplines without the user's actual region. Use current official sources. If suicide or self-harm support is needed, use the available official helpline lookup mechanism before naming a service.

Do not diagnose. Recommend professional support when sleep, eating, concentration, study/work, panic, substance use, compulsive checking, stalking impulses, self-harm or suicidal thinking materially impair function.

For irreversible public exposure, contacting employers/family/new partners, aggressive messages or publication of private conversations, default to a delay and reassess evidence, objective, benefit, risk and irreversibility unless immediate safety requires action.
