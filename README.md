# Breakup Master — Beta

**Breakup Master** is a bilingual Relationship Intelligence Skill for dating, ambiguous relationships, commitment decisions, established relationships, escalation, crises, breakups, reconciliation and recovery.

It does not default to “leave them” or “get them back.” It reconstructs evidence first, models both people independently, accounts for culture, geography, family systems, LGBTQ+ context and life stage, then separates relationship worth, difficulty and real-world feasibility. When several explanations remain plausible, it gives calibrated probabilities rather than pretending certainty.

It also includes a multi-framework psychology engine drawing on attachment research, EFT, Gottman research, family systems, emotion regulation, rejection/social pain, grief, self-compassion, boundaries and desire/commitment frameworks. Strong psychological claims should identify the relevant framework and distinguish research evidence from clinical interpretation or popular psychology.

## Languages

Chinese and English are both first-class output languages. The skill follows the user’s dominant language unless explicitly asked for Chinese, English or bilingual output. Advice is localized to the user’s region and cultural context.

## Scope

- Dating / ambiguous relationships / situationships
- Whether to start or formalize a relationship
- Whether to continue or escalate a relationship
- Family, money, sex, culture, distance and migration issues
- LGBTQ+ relationship contexts
- Betrayal, wrongdoing and safety issues
- Breakups and reconciliation
- Recovery / moving on
- Revenge as accountability and consequence design, not harmful retaliation
- Mental-health support triggers and local-resource routing

## Status

**Public Beta.** This is a decision-support and relationship-analysis skill, not psychotherapy, medical diagnosis, emergency care, or legal representation.
